# tarang.dev v8: A day at the desk

One world. The home page is a WebGL desk you scroll through as a single working day, 09:00 to 23:59.
Every fact lives in `src/lib/content.ts` and renders as static HTML; the 3D scene is a layer on top.

## The day (`src/components/desk/`)
`DeskScene.tsx` pins a 1700svh section. Scroll progress drives the time (`timeline.ts → hourAt`), the camera and the screen on the monitor.

| Stop | Time | Screen (`steps/`) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Boot | 09:00 | Lock screen, POST log, intro |
| Plan | 09:30 | Standup board from `now.items`, learning column |
| Commit | 10:30 | `git log --graph` walking the five chapters |
| Build | 12:30 | Editor walking all projects, live preview, build log |
| Learn | 13:30 | Browser tabs for what I'm learning, toolbox sidebar |
| Review | 15:00 | Seven questions answered, with sources |
| Debug | 16:30 | Two real fixes as issue, diff and result |
| Deploy | 18:00 | Deploy board, stats, app downloads |
| Ship | 21:30 | A real email / WhatsApp composer wired to the 3D keyboard |

- Scrolling is deliberately slow: Lenis `lerp 0.07`, `wheelMultiplier 0.75`, no snapping. The rail jumps to any stop.
- Phones get a mini 3D desk with a "drag the day" slider plus a swipeable deck of the nine screens.
- Reduced motion or no WebGL: the nine screens stacked as cards.

## The scene (`src/components/desk/three/`)
React Three Fiber, all procedural: no model files.
- `props.tsx`: walnut desk, monitor (bezel, aluminium stand, clearcoat glass), keyboard with printed legends,
  sculpted rows and an underglow tinted per stop, architect lamp with springs and a real spotlight,
  plant with curved leaves, ceramic mug with steam, phone with live notifications, books, notebook, clock, shelf props.
- `textures.ts`: canvas-generated wood, plaster, planks, keycap legend atlas, book spines, phone screen.
- `SkyMaterial.ts` + `daylight.ts`: the window sky, sun, moon, stars and city lights follow the hour.
- The monitor's screens are live HTML via drei `<Html transform>`, portalled into a stable layer.
- Quality tiers: high (shadows, MSAA, N8AO, contact shadows, legends) or low (dpr 1, none of those).
  `PerformanceMonitor` drops to low on slow GPUs; `?gl=low` forces it.
